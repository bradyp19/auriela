# Aurelia backend inheritance investigation

Investigated October 13, 2026. Scope: locate historical agent/backend, establish Stripe's role and flight booking/ticketing behavior, and identify reusable code for the agreed [first-flight journey](../product/first-flight-journey.md). This is a discovery report, not an architectural or booking-ownership decision. [Glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md) terminology remains authoritative.

## Findings

**The historical backend was not located within the accessible repositories or pertinent Notion sources.** Both inherited GitHub repositories contain the same October 9 demo commit. The historical documents refer to a different worker/core/web layout. Therefore this report cannot establish the historical Stripe handlers, supplier calls, ticket issuance, deployment, or live transaction behavior.

**Stripe is explicitly intended for Pro subscriptions in the historical documents.** Travel checkout is described separately through provider forms, with a Duffel payment intent for flights. No inspected evidence establishes Stripe airfare collection, assistance fees, combined travel/subscription charges, authorization/capture timing, or refunds. Actual Stripe charges remain unknown. [master-sprint-doc](https://app.notion.com/p/38e46a31b58a80598549c501130ab4b7), [V1 Core Features](https://app.notion.com/p/39746a31b58a80b69a24eed28ba4c5bd).

**The accessible code collects no payment and issues no flight ticket.** Booking records are explicitly demo records with `paymentState: 'not-charged'`; flight details are planning-only and the checkout UI is restricted to hotels. These conclusions apply to this inspected implementation, not every historical company system. [demo provider](https://github.com/pbelay1/auriela/blob/1ae4659ff5c865b9fc748fcb01970124cfe224c8/src/providers/demo.ts#L44-L56), [flight details and checkout controls](https://github.com/pbelay1/auriela/blob/1ae4659ff5c865b9fc748fcb01970124cfe224c8/src/components/details.tsx#L31-L35).

## Reuse assessment for the agreed flight journey

These are inspected reuse candidates, not a recommendation to keep or replace the entire codebase:

- **Presentation and small domain utilities:** price-breakdown rendering, integer-cent USD formatting, calendar-date helpers and trip snapshot/context-change logic are reasonable candidates. Existing tests describe their local behavior. Flight-aware itinerary/terms presentation still needs supplier data. [money](https://github.com/pbelay1/auriela/blob/1ae4659ff5c865b9fc748fcb01970124cfe224c8/src/domain/money.ts), [dates](https://github.com/pbelay1/auriela/blob/1ae4659ff5c865b9fc748fcb01970124cfe224c8/src/domain/dates.ts), [trip logic](https://github.com/pbelay1/auriela/blob/1ae4659ff5c865b9fc748fcb01970124cfe224c8/src/application/trips.ts), [tests](https://github.com/pbelay1/auriela/blob/1ae4659ff5c865b9fc748fcb01970124cfe224c8/tests/domain.test.ts).
- **Provider separation:** search/refresh/book/cancel interfaces identify useful boundaries, but current types lock quotes/bookings to `demo:true`, USD, simplified states and hotel-centric criteria. They are a starting vocabulary, not production-compatible contracts. [contracts](https://github.com/pbelay1/auriela/blob/1ae4659ff5c865b9fc748fcb01970124cfe224c8/src/providers/contracts.ts), [types](https://github.com/pbelay1/auriela/blob/1ae4659ff5c865b9fc748fcb01970124cfe224c8/src/domain/types.ts).
- **Demo scenarios and local storage:** useful for interaction examples, but hardcoded prices/terms, a volatile duplicate Map and browser localStorage cannot establish supplier integration, durable booking recovery or traveler isolation. [demo](https://github.com/pbelay1/auriela/blob/1ae4659ff5c865b9fc748fcb01970124cfe224c8/src/providers/demo.ts), [storage](https://github.com/pbelay1/auriela/blob/1ae4659ff5c865b9fc748fcb01970124cfe224c8/src/providers/storage.ts).
- **Historical agent:** Notion's `scope.ts`, `PreferenceStore`, shared tables and payment-session references are promising search leads only. Reuse quality cannot be assessed until actual source, revision and isolation/recovery behavior are inspected. [V1](https://app.notion.com/p/39746a31b58a80b69a24eed28ba4c5bd), [Sprint 17](https://app.notion.com/p/38e46a31b58a8019ae39e86311ef78dc).

Specific unverified capabilities for launch remain approved domestic-flight supplier access/coverage; actual offer refresh and terms; durable specific approval; payment-to-order association; ticket confirmation; duplicate/uncertain-outcome recovery; cancellation/refund servicing; SMS/WhatsApp identity/preferences; and departure reminders. This is an evidence gap list, not authorization to rewrite those capabilities. Do not substitute the demonstration's broader hotel/group/trip scope for the agreed one-adult first-flight journey.

## Evidence required next and booking ownership decision

First obtain the historical repository URL and readable ref, former CTO's GitHub owner/organization, or a source archive location. The available repositories do not supply that identity. Then inspect actual checkout line items and modes, signed webhook handling, supplier request/response parsing, persistent approval/attempt/order records, funding/capture/refund timing, and ticket-verification/recovery logic. Sanitized deployment revision plus completed test/live booking and servicing evidence is needed to establish operation; no credentials or raw customer/payment records belong in this report.

Q2 remains open: **Will Aurelia own the approved-offer purchase and post-booking servicing workflow, or will a supplier/partner own checkout, ticketing and servicing after a traveler handoff?** A supplier payment form can still be embedded in an Aurelia-owned orchestration flow; who processes the card does not alone settle purchase/support responsibility. Whichever model is selected must specify who verifies ticket issuance, investigates uncertain outcomes, handles cancellations/refunds/changes and supports the traveler. Historical intent leans toward Aurelia orchestration, but that is an inference from the documents, not an adopted decision or proven capability. [V1](https://app.notion.com/p/39746a31b58a80b69a24eed28ba4c5bd), [master sprint](https://app.notion.com/p/38e46a31b58a80598549c501130ab4b7).

Update October 13, 2026: the user selected embedded supplier booking to support ease of use. This resolves the customer-facing booking model: Aurelia coordinates the experience and the supplier manages payment/ticketing. Supplier selection, account approval, contractual payment/funding arrangements and direct versus relayed support remain open. The agreed decision is recorded in [the first-flight journey](../product/first-flight-journey.md). Earlier findings and the Q2 question above retain the investigation context; they are not the current decision status.

## Verification and limits

### Follow-up: existing Nuitée / LiteAPI keys

The user reports having Nuitée and LiteAPI keys. Their values, account entitlements and live configuration have not been inspected. Current official documentation changes the available design options, without changing the findings about historical implementation:

- LiteAPI's documentation now describes the platform as Nuitee Connect. Flights uses the same authentication/API key as hotels; possession of a hotel key does not prove production flight approval. Production flights and whitelabel flights require explicit enablement. [Getting started](https://docs.liteapi.travel/reference/getting-started-1), [flight access](https://docs.liteapi.travel/docs/getting-access-to-flights).
- A flight prebook can return a supplier-created Stripe payment intent, followed by payment confirmation and completion of the booking. Stripe in a travel checkout therefore need not mean an airfare charge through Aurelia's own Stripe account. The old Notion subscription references do not resolve actual travel payment routing. [flight prebook](https://docs.liteapi.travel/reference/post_flights-prebooks).
- Nuitée documents embedded Stripe payments with Nuitée as merchant of record, plus airline servicing through either direct traveler support (First Line) or cases relayed by the app's team (B2B-Relayed). This permits an Aurelia booking experience with supplier payment/servicing ownership; the earlier two-way question was too coarse. The documentation also describes credit-line settlement for servicing, so exact funding and charges need account/contract confirmation. [flight support and billing](https://docs.liteapi.travel/docs/flights-support-billing-model).
- For hotels, account-card funding explicitly supports an app collecting payment through its own gateway and then paying the supplier with its account card. That is a separate arrangement from supplier-managed traveler payments. [account-card payment](https://docs.liteapi.travel/docs/account-credit-card).

Provisional recommendation for a sole founding engineer: first investigate whether the existing account supports production flights, supplier-managed traveler payments and direct Nuitée servicing. Retain Aurelia's messaging, specific-offer approval and confirmation tracking. This recommendation is not an adopted product decision, verified account capability or authorization to start implementation. The key names suggest a supplier platform to evaluate; they do not select the payment/support arrangement. No keys were read and no API booking or prebook was created in this follow-up.
