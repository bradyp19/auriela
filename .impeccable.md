## Design Context

### Users
Travelers searching, comparing and planning trips in one saved workspace. Phase One is a transparent, locally persisted demonstration for frontend review and backend integration.

### Brand Personality
Hyperminimalist, sleek, futuristic. Calm motion and precise controls should build confidence.

### Aesthetic Direction
The user requests DM Sans exclusively; black, white, charcoal and silver UI; a black globe hero and a light working canvas with a dark composer. The three screenshots mentioned in the brief were not included in the supplied attachments.

### Design Principles
- One trip connects search, shortlist, itinerary and booking.
- Generous space and clear type carry the hierarchy.
- Monochrome controls frame natural travel photography.
- Motion communicates actual work and respects reduced motion.
- Demo boundaries, prices and conditions are visible and honest.
